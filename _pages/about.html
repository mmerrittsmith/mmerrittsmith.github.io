{% fullwidth 'images/img/IMG_3202.jpg' '' %} I am a PhD student at Berkeley in the Information School, where I'm lucky to be advised by Josh Blumenstock. You can find my school webpage here. I'm interested in using econometrics and machine learning methods to improve the lives of disadvantaged people around the world, especially focusing on finding out how to most effectively target interventions. I have previously focused on research on crime, policing, development, deep learning, and causal inference. I hold an MS in Computational Analysis and Public Policy from the University of Chicago, and a BA in Data Science and Public Policy from Tufts University.
