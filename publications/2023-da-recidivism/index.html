Criminal charges, risk assessment, and violent recidivism in cases of domestic abuse

Published in NBER Working Paper Series, 2023

I contributed to this paper as an RA while at Harris. It studies domestic abuse recidivism using data from the Greater Manchester Police. In this project, we studied two treatments: charging the perpetrator with a crime, or using a formal risk assessment created by the police to determine if the perpetrator should be labeled as high-risk. I worked on the propensity score weighting and analyzed the treatments for heterogeneity.
