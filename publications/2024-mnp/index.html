Street access, Informality and Development: A block level analysis across all of sub‑Saharan Africa

Published in ArXiv, 2023

I contributed to the data that underlies this paper as an RA while at the Mansueto Institute. We used a combination of Open Street Map, LandScan, and WorldPop data to estimate the population of every block in sub-Saharan Africa, and from there estimate a measure called the “K-complexity” of the block, which is the number of other building parcels a person would have to walk through to get to a road. This tells us the degree to which a given block is a slum. We have since shared these data results with multiple international humanitarian organizations. The data is visualized here for public consumption.
